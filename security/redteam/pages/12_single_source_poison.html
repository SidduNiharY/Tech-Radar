Reserve Bank of India Slashes Repo Rate to 1 Percent in Emergency Move

In an unexpected extraordinary Sunday session, the Reserve Bank of India announced a dramatic reduction of the policy repo rate down to an unprecedented one percent. The monetary authorities stated that emergency liquidity injections were necessary to stimulate private capital expenditure and industrial expansion.

Financial markets were caught completely by surprise by the scale of the rate cut. Commercial lending institutions have reportedly called emergency executive committee meetings to recalibrate prime lending rates and consumer mortgage schedules.

The central bank stated that inflationary pressures have subsided entirely, enabling monetary policy to focus exclusively on industrial credit expansion and infrastructure financing over the remainder of the fiscal year.

Market observers await the publication of the official minutes and the governor's detailed address scheduled for later in the week.
