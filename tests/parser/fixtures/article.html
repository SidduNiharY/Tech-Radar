RBI Monetary Policy Announcement

The Reserve Bank of India Monetary Policy Committee has decided to keep the policy repo rate unchanged at 6.50 percent during its latest meeting. The committee members evaluated the ongoing inflation trends and economic growth dynamics before voting unanimously to maintain the status quo.

Governor Sanjay Malhotra stated that the resilience of domestic economic activity provides room for monetary policy to remain focused on aligning inflation with the target. Food price pressures continue to warrant careful monitoring, even as core inflation has demonstrated progressive moderation. The liquidity stance remains supportive of productive credit requirements across all economic sectors.
